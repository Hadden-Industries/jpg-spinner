JPG Spinner - retained licence and notice inputs

This development candidate is not cleared for public distribution. These files
preserve selected licence and notice inputs; they do not replace the exact
corresponding source, dependency terms review or release approval.

The application's GNU General Public License version 3 is in LICENSE.txt.
Third-party terms remain in the original files under Licenses. They are not
relicensed by the application licence. Original vendor text, byte order marks
and line endings are preserved rather than replaced by short licence labels.

This software is based in part on the work of the Independent JPEG Group.

Licenses/native contains the retained copyright assets for the resolved
adobe-xmp-core, exiv2, expat, inih, libjpeg-turbo, libspng and zlib build inputs,
plus libjpeg-turbo's complete upstream README.ijg. These are conservative
build-context notices, not proof that every installed library contributes code
to the executable. Catch2 is test-only and is not included in this application
notice set.

Licenses/Microsoft.Windows.ImplementationLibrary.LICENSE.txt and Licenses/nuget
contain the exact retained notice inputs from the resolved Microsoft package
graph. C++/WinRT and WIL supply generated/header inputs; the Windows App Runtime
is a separate framework dependency. Resolved WebView2, AI/ML and SDK/build-tool
notices are retained conservatively, not as a claim that their entire packages
are redistributed here or that the app implements those features. Identical
notice payloads shared by multiple Windows App SDK packages are stored once.

Microsoft.Windows.SDK.BuildTools 10.0.28000.2705 supplies an external terms URL,
https://aka.ms/WinSDKLicenseURL, rather than an embedded licence file. Its exact
applicable terms remain unresolved; another Microsoft package's licence does
not resolve them. Final binary contribution, complete notice sufficiency,
GPL/Exiv2 compatibility, corresponding-source delivery and current Store terms
still require qualification before any release. No source offer is made by
this development index, and no process/linkage boundary is treated as an
exemption from licence obligations.
